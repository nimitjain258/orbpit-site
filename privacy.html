Privacy Policy

ORBPIT and the GameSim app · Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information ORBPIT, the 24/7 YouTube live stream, and GameSim, the software that runs it, use, and why. ORBPIT uses YouTube API Services. By watching or taking part in the stream you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The GameSim app and Google sign-in

GameSim signs in with Google to one account only: the ORBPIT YouTube channel, which belongs to the operator of this stream. With that permission it:

  • lists the ORBPIT channel's own live broadcasts, to find the one currently streaming and its live chat;
  • starts a waiting ORBPIT broadcast when the stream restarts, so the show stays on air unattended.

It does not access any other channel or account, does not upload, edit or delete videos, and does not read private data. The sign-in token is stored only on the computer that runs the stream. It is never shared and never sent anywhere except to Google.

Viewers who take part in chat

GameSim reads the stream's public live chat through the YouTube API, along with the public details YouTube attaches to each message: the sender's display name, YouTube channel ID, and whether they are a channel member or subscriber. It uses these to:

  • run chat commands such as !join and !predict: put your orb in a fight and show your display name on screen;
  • keep the game's leaderboards, such as correct predictions;
  • know who has chatted recently, because occasional on-stream prizes are drawn from recently active viewers.

The text of chat messages is not stored. What is stored is a small game record per viewer: display name, channel ID, member or subscriber status, when they last chatted, and their game results. These records are kept on the computer that runs the stream. They are not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

A viewer who has chatted but has no leaderboard result is removed automatically after 24 hours of inactivity. Leaderboard records are kept for as long as the game runs, so returning players keep their standing. To have your records removed, email the address below with your YouTube display name and channel link, and they will be deleted within 30 days.

The channel owner can withdraw GameSim's access to the ORBPIT channel at any time from the Google account permissions page. Doing so stops all access by the app.

Children

ORBPIT does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Participation follows YouTube's own age requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions or deletion requests, contact orbpit.business@gmail.com.
